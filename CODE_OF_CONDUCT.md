# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We are committed to making the AI Engineer Pack a welcoming, respectful, and productive project for everyone. This repository exists to help people and AI coding assistants work more safely, clearly, and effectively. Contributors are expected to collaborate in a way that reflects that purpose.

## Expected behavior
Examples of behavior that strengthens this community include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Giving feedback that is specific, respectful, and actionable.
- Focusing criticism on content, behavior, evidence, and outcomes rather than people.
- Respecting different levels of experience with AI tools, software engineering, documentation, and open source contribution.
- Being transparent about assumptions, limitations, and uncertainty.
- Helping maintain professional quality in prompts, workflows, templates, and checklists.

## Unacceptable behavior
Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or attention.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately submitting misleading, unsafe, malicious, or low-quality guidance.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer feedback or project contribution standards.
- Using issues, pull requests, or discussions to promote unrelated services or spam.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, documentation review, and any public communication representing the AI Engineer Pack project.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the maintainers through the repository's supported contact channel listed in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Include relevant links, screenshots, dates, and a concise description of the behavior when possible.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

1. Clarifying expectations or requesting edits.
2. Removing comments, issues, pull requests, or content.
3. Temporarily limiting participation.
4. Permanently banning a participant from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions will consider severity, pattern of behavior, impact on the community, and whether the participant acts in good faith to correct the issue.

## Contributor guidance
Before contributing, review [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). The contribution guide explains project standards for documentation quality, folder structure, examples, and review expectations.
